# SmartGuide System Tour Script (5–7 minutes)

**Audience:** panel, instructors, or visitors  
**Duration:** 5–7 minutes  
**Target pace:** ~6 minutes if you run one live sensor + one emergency  
**Props:** SmartGuide app (start **logged out**), Smart Cane, Smart Eyeglass, demo guardian account ready  

Use **SAY** for spoken lines. Use **DO** for what you show. Keep pauses to 2–3 seconds after each demo beat.

---

## Setup (before you start)

- Start on the welcome screen (not signed in). Know the demo email/password.
- Cane paired; both devices **Online** after you sign in.
- Home map, Status, and Alerts one tap away after login.
- Object ready for cane ultrasonic and for glasses “ahead.”
- Decide: live **SOS** or only explain it (SOS adds ~40 seconds).

If a device is offline, say: “No heartbeat for about 45 seconds, so it shows offline. Last GPS stays on the map.”

---

## 1. Opening — 30 seconds

**DO:** Hold cane, then glasses, then show the SmartGuide welcome screen.

**SAY:**

> Good day. This is **SmartGuide**. Three parts work together: the **Smart Cane**, the **Smart Eyeglass**, and the **guardian app**.
>
> The cane watches the path and location. The glasses watch what is ahead and support voice. The app lets family see live GPS, device health, and emergencies.
>
> Safer walking for the user. Faster help when something goes wrong.

---

## 2. Log in, sign in, Google — 50 seconds

**DO:** Stay on the first screen. Point to **Log in**, **Create account**, and **Continue with Google**.

**SAY:**

> The guardian enters here. **Log in** if they already have an account. **Create account** if they are new. **Continue with Google** is the fast path — one tap, same Google they already use.

**DO:** Tap **Log in**. Show **Sign in**: email, password, **Sign In**, Forgot password, then **Google** at the bottom.

**SAY:**

> Sign in is email and password, then **Sign In**. They can also use **Google** on this screen.

**DO:** Go back. Tap **Create account**. Glance at full name, phone, email, password, **Sign Up**, and **Sign up with Google**. Do not fill a new account live unless you have extra time.

**SAY:**

> New guardians register with name, phone, email, and password, or **Sign up with Google**. After that they land on the map.

**DO:** Return to Sign in (or welcome **Continue with Google**). Sign in with the demo account — email **Sign In**, or **Continue with Google** if that is the demo. Wait for Home.

If Google is slow, use email **Sign In**. Do not debug Google on stage.

---

## 3. Smart Cane — 70 seconds

**DO:** Point at the cane. Open **Status** on the selected cane.

**SAY:**

> The cane is the walking aid and the GPS beacon. Ultrasonic detects obstacles at cane height. PIR detects nearby motion. GPS sends the live pin you see on the map. Battery, fall, and an **SOS** button complete it.
>
> When ultrasonic fires, the user is warned on the device, and the guardian sees **Obstacle** on Alerts. Fall or SOS is an emergency: a high-priority notification and an Alerts card with the cane name and place.

**DO:** Block the ultrasonic sensor. Show Status → Ultrasonic **Detected**. Open **Alerts** → Obstacle. Return to Home.

Skip PIR as a second live demo unless ultrasonic fails.

---

## 4. Smart Eyeglass — 50 seconds

**DO:** Status → scroll to **Eyeglass**.

**SAY:**

> The cane cannot see head-height obstacles. The glasses cover **what is ahead** and voice. They pair as the same Cane ID plus `-EYEGLASS`, so Status shows a second connection, voice command, obstacles ahead, and a separate battery.
>
> If the glasses drop, the cane still tracks GPS. If the cane drops, the glasses can still report ahead-obstacles. Each device is **Online** only when that unit is on Wi-Fi.

**DO:** Object in front of the glasses. Show **Obstacles Ahead → Ahead**. Glance at the map banner.

---

## 5. SmartGuide app — 80 seconds

**DO:** Home. Expand the map status banner.

**SAY:**

> Home is the operations screen: cane pin, guardian phone, battery, eyeglass online, and the place name — barangay and city, not raw coordinates.

**DO:** Status — list only, then one details peek. Do not re-read every row.

**SAY:**

> Status is where we add a cane — username, Cane ID, contact number — and watch both devices. Route history is the recent trail.

**DO:** Directions: show Drive / Motorcycle / Walk, then **Go**, then End Go immediately.

**SAY:**

> If the user needs help, the guardian routes from their phone to the cane and taps **Go**.

**DO:** Alerts — point to Emergencies vs Sensors. Profile — one second.

**SAY:**

> Alerts split **fall and SOS** from obstacle and motion. Profile is the guardian account they just signed in with.

---

## 6. One loop + close — 50 seconds

**SAY:**

> One story: guardian signs in — email or Google. User walks with cane and glasses. Ground obstacle — cane warns, app shows Obstacle. Something ahead — glasses mark Ahead. Fall or SOS — family gets the emergency, opens the map, taps **Go**, and travels to them.
>
> Sense. Warn the user. Notify the family. Navigate to help. That is SmartGuide.
>
> Thank you. We can take questions.

**DO (only if you still have time before 7:00):** press SOS, show the notification and the Emergency Request card.

---

## Cut if you are over time

1. Skip Create account; show Log in + **Continue with Google** only, then Sign In.  
2. Skip PIR, Route History, Profile.  
3. Skip live SOS; describe it in the closing story.  
4. Do not re-open Status after the glasses beat.

---

## Fast Q&A (10 seconds each)

**How do they get in?** Log in with email, or **Continue with Google**. New users Create account or Sign up with Google.

**Why two devices?** Cane = path + GPS. Glasses = ahead + voice.

**Who uses the app?** The guardian. The user gets feedback from the hardware.

**Emergency vs sensor?** Fall and SOS are emergencies. Obstacle and motion are sensors.

**Offline?** Last GPS stays. Live sensors pause until the heartbeat returns.
